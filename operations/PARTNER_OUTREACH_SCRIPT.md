# Partner Outreach Script

## Realtor Angle

Subject: Stop losing buyers after lender fallout

Hi {{name}},

I am building a difficult-file approval desk for buyers who get declined, delayed, or told to come back later.

The goal is simple: when a buyer falls out because of credit, DTI, cash-to-close, self-employment income, DPA, or documentation problems, we dissect the file and give you a clear next path instead of letting the buyer disappear.

For the pilot, I am reviewing a limited number of declined or stuck buyer files and returning:

- the main approval obstacle
- a fundability status
- a 30/60/90 day action plan
- possible lender, DPA, or agency paths

No approval guarantees and no document games. Just a cleaner way to save difficult buyers.

Can I review 3 to 5 of your stuck buyer files for the pilot?

## Builder Angle

Subject: Recover more buyer fallout from financing problems

Hi {{name}},

I am launching a fallout recovery desk for builders whose buyers fail financing because of credit, DTI, cash-to-close, DPA, or documentation issues.

Instead of marking those buyers dead, we classify the file, identify the fastest lawful path, and track the buyer until they are fundable now, fundable with another path, or placed on a build plan.

For the pilot, I am looking for one builder team willing to test the workflow on recent financing fallout.

I can return a weekly report showing:

- files reviewed
- buyer obstacle types
- likely save paths
- buyers ready for lender routing
- buyers needing 30/60/90 day build plans

Would you be open to testing this on your recent fallout list?
