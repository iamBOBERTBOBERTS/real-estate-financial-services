# Lender Discovery Questions

Use these questions to build the real lender/program matrix.

## Identity and Coverage

- What states do you lend in?
- Are you a lender, broker, bank, credit union, housing agency, or DPA administrator?
- Which borrower profiles do you actively want more of?
- Which borrower profiles should not be sent to you?

## Credit Appetite

- What are your practical minimum score bands by product?
- Do you work below 620?
- Do you work below 580?
- Are thin-file borrowers workable?
- How do you handle recent lates, collections, charge-offs, and disputed accounts?

## DTI and Manual Underwriting

- Do you support manual underwriting?
- Which products allow manual underwriting in your shop?
- What DTI levels are realistically workable with compensating factors?
- Which compensating factors matter most to you?
- How do you treat payment shock and rental history?

## Cash-to-Close and DPA

- Which DPA programs do you support?
- Can DPA stack with seller credits?
- Can DPA stack with lender credits?
- What reserve requirements commonly kill files?
- What documentation do you need for gift funds and source of funds?

## Income

- How do you treat self-employed borrowers?
- What tax return losses are fatal?
- Do you offer bank statement, P&L, asset depletion, or other alternative-doc products?
- How do you treat 1099, gig, seasonal, commission, bonus, and overtime income?
- What history or seasoning is required?

## Property

- Do you lend on manufactured homes?
- Do you lend on condos with project issues?
- What property condition issues are usually fatal?
- Do you support renovation or repair-escrow options?

## Operations

- What is your average response time on a scenario?
- Who receives scenario packages?
- What information should be included in a first scenario?
- Do you provide written scenario feedback?
- What causes files to stall in your process?

## Compliance and Compensation

- What referral or partner arrangements do you permit?
- What disclosures do you require?
- What activities should ApprovalPath OS avoid to prevent licensing or RESPA issues?
- Can you review a sample routing package before pilot referrals?
