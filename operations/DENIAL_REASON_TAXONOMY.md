# Denial Reason Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy to normalize borrower fallout reasons.

## Credit

- Low score
- Thin file
- Recent late payments
- Collections
- Charge-offs
- Judgments or liens
- Bankruptcy waiting period
- Foreclosure waiting period
- Disputed accounts
- Credit utilization too high

## Capacity

- DTI too high
- Residual income too low
- Payment shock too high
- Student loan calculation issue
- Installment debt burden
- Revolving debt burden
- Co-borrower income unavailable

## Capital

- Insufficient down payment
- Insufficient closing costs
- Insufficient reserves
- Gift funds unavailable or undocumented
- Source of funds unclear
- DPA unavailable
- Seller credit unavailable

## Income

- Self-employed income too low
- 1099 income not seasoned
- Variable income not usable
- Overtime/bonus/commission not usable
- Employment gap
- Cash income unsupported
- Tax return losses
- Bank statement deposits inconsistent

## Collateral

- Appraisal value issue
- Property condition issue
- Condo/project issue
- Manufactured home issue
- Property type not eligible
- Repair requirement
- Occupancy concern

## Documentation

- Missing paystubs
- Missing W-2s
- Missing tax returns
- Missing bank statements
- Missing denial letter
- Missing AUS findings
- Inconsistent borrower statements
- Incomplete explanation letters

## Compliance or Fraud Risk

- Identity mismatch
- Occupancy inconsistency
- Undocumented funds
- Altered or suspicious documents
- Undisclosed debt
- Undisclosed property ownership
- Conflicting income documents
