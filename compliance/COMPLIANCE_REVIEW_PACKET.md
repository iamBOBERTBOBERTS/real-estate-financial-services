# Compliance Review Packet

Purpose: give a mortgage compliance attorney a clear review package before the pilot moves into paid or referral-based activity.

## Business Summary

ApprovalPath OS is intended to provide file review, education, document organization, approval-path planning, and lender/program matching support for difficult mortgage files.

The initial model is not intended to originate loans, take mortgage applications, negotiate loan terms, guarantee approval, or sell settlement-service referrals.

## Items for Legal Review

1. Whether borrower intake fields or conversations trigger mortgage broker or MLO licensing.
2. Whether the consumer-paid diagnostic fee is permissible in the target launch states.
3. Whether realtor, builder, or partner subscriptions create RESPA Section 8 risk.
4. Whether lender routing or partner matching creates steering, advertising, or referral disclosure obligations.
5. Whether credit-related advice triggers credit repair or credit services organization rules.
6. Whether housing-counseling language or activity creates HUD counseling requirements.
7. Whether privacy and data-security controls meet applicable GLBA/Safeguards expectations.
8. Whether the consent language is sufficient for borrower data sharing.
9. Whether state-specific mortgage, data privacy, and consumer protection rules apply.
10. Whether marketing claims are safe.

## High-Risk Phrases to Avoid

- Guaranteed approval
- Everyone qualifies
- We can get anyone approved
- Secret lender loopholes
- We repair your credit
- No documentation needed
- We erase bad credit
- Pay us and get approved

## Preferred Safe Language

- No approval guarantees
- File review and education
- Approval-path planning
- Lender/program matching based on borrower profile
- Consent-based sharing
- Lawful documentation only
- Subject to lender underwriting and program rules
