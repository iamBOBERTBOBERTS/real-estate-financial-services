# Business Model Basis

## Working Concept

The business should focus on hard-to-approve homebuyers: people other lenders decline, delay, pre-screen out, or tell to come back later. The core idea is not ordinary mortgage lead generation. It is an approval engineering and mortgage rescue model that rebuilds difficult borrower files, matches them to the right lending path, and routes them to capable lenders, agencies, or programs.

Working name from the source conversation:

**The Impossible Approval Desk**

Alternative category language:

- ApprovalPath OS
- Homebuyer Recovery OS
- Mortgage Second Opinion Network
- Buyer Recovery Platform
- Fundability Engine

## Core Promise

Public-facing promise:

**We specialize in buyers other lenders say cannot be approved. We rebuild the file, find the right program, stack every lawful advantage, and get the buyer to the lender most likely to approve that exact profile.**

Compliance-safe version:

**No approval guarantees. No fake documents. No steering. No credit games. We maximize lawful approval probability through file engineering, program matching, DPA stacking, and lender routing.**

## Strategic Thesis

Most lenders are optimized for clean borrowers who already fit the box. This business is built for people with complicated files.

The opportunity is to own the layer above individual lenders:

- Diagnose why the borrower failed.
- Classify the file by approval obstacle.
- Build a lawful requalification path.
- Match the borrower to the right loan type, DPA program, nonprofit, housing agency, credit union, broker, portfolio lender, or non-QM lender.
- Keep the buyer, realtor, builder, and lender relationship alive while the buyer becomes fundable.

The goal is to outperform a single strong local lender by aggregating many lender capabilities and becoming the specialist workflow for difficult files.

## Target Borrower Profiles

The model should be built around these high-friction cases:

- Low credit score
- High debt-to-income ratio
- No down payment
- Cash-to-close shortage
- Thin credit file
- Self-employed income
- 1099, gig, or variable income
- Prior mortgage denial
- Recent credit events or derogatory history
- Manual-underwrite candidate
- Property condition or appraisal issues
- Borrowers needing down payment assistance
- Borrowers needing FHA, VA, USDA, portfolio, non-QM, bank statement, asset-based, or alternative documentation paths

## Product Shape

The product should work like an approval lab:

1. Intake the borrower file with consent.
2. Capture denial reason or current approval obstacle.
3. Classify the borrower into an approval path.
4. Build a missing-document and compensating-factor checklist.
5. Identify lawful fixes such as DPA, gift funds, seller credit, lender credit, reserve planning, debt payoff sequencing, income documentation cleanup, or program rerouting.
6. Match to the lender or agency most likely to handle that profile.
7. Track progress until the file becomes approved, paused, or ineligible.

## Guardrails

This business must be built with strong compliance boundaries:

- Do not guarantee approval.
- Do not create, alter, or encourage false documents.
- Do not imply credit repair outcomes that are not lawful or achievable.
- Do not sell referral placements as objective recommendations.
- Do not accept illegal referral fees or kickbacks for settlement services.
- Use written consent before collecting, storing, or sharing borrower information.
- Treat borrower data as sensitive financial information.
- Separate education, matching, lead routing, loan origination, and compensation models clearly.
- Get licensing and legal review before taking applications, negotiating loan terms, or receiving compensation in ways that may trigger mortgage broker or MLO rules.

## Competitive Insight From Sunflower Bank Review

The source conversation framed Sunflower Bank and Caitlin Schulzke as a strong example of local mortgage execution, not as a secret approval loophole. Their apparent strengths:

- Local originator credibility
- Realtor and builder referral capture
- Fast pre-qualification and pre-underwriting messaging
- Down payment assistance awareness
- Broad loan product routing
- Portfolio and non-QM options
- High-touch communication
- Mortgage app and calculators
- Bank-backed infrastructure

The business should not merely copy this. It should become the difficult-file specialist that can route across many lender boxes instead of being limited to one institution.

## Near-Term Build Direction

The first practical build should be an internal operating system for difficult borrower files:

- Borrower intake profile
- Denial reason taxonomy
- File obstacle scoring
- Program and lender matching rules
- DPA and assistance tracker
- Document checklist generator
- Realtor/builder referral tracking
- Consent and disclosure workflow
- CRM pipeline by rescue stage
- Outcome tracking by borrower profile and lender/program path

The early version can be manual behind the scenes while the system learns which lender and program paths work best for each borrower type.
