# Consent and Disclosure Draft

This draft is for legal review before use.

## Service Disclosure

ApprovalPath OS provides file review, education, document organization, approval-path planning, and lender/program matching support for difficult mortgage files.

ApprovalPath OS is not guaranteeing loan approval, loan terms, interest rates, underwriting outcomes, credit score improvement, down payment assistance eligibility, or property eligibility.

Unless separately licensed and disclosed, ApprovalPath OS does not act as a mortgage lender, mortgage broker, loan originator, credit repair organization, housing counselor, attorney, tax advisor, or financial advisor.

## Borrower Consent

I authorize ApprovalPath OS to review the information and documents I provide for the purpose of evaluating potential mortgage readiness, approval obstacles, assistance options, and possible lender or program paths.

I authorize ApprovalPath OS to share my information with the specific lenders, brokers, housing agencies, assistance programs, or service providers I approve in writing.

I understand that I may revoke this authorization in writing, subject to any actions already taken based on my prior authorization.

## Data Handling Notice

Borrower information may include sensitive personal and financial information. ApprovalPath OS will use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect borrower information and will limit access to people or providers involved in the file review or approved referral process.

## Referral Disclosure

ApprovalPath OS may identify lenders, brokers, agencies, programs, or service providers that appear to fit the borrower profile. A match or recommendation does not guarantee approval.

Any compensation, business relationship, or material connection involving a recommended provider should be disclosed clearly before referral or submission.

## No Fraud or Document Alteration

ApprovalPath OS will not create, alter, submit, or encourage false, misleading, or unsupported borrower information. Borrowers are responsible for providing accurate and complete information.
